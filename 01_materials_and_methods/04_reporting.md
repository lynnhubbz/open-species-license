## Part C: Reporting

- **Sankey Diagram**
- **Aspect Chart:** one 100% bar per aspect, split by code. This shows Steps 2 and 4 visually.
- Aspect Table 
- **Species Table:** each species with its: 
  - source, 
  - information, 
  - classification (Open / Semi-Open / Insufficient), and 
  - possible license, 
  - access date.
- **Honest note:** where the base license is more permissive than most species (like NSFW), say so. It's a deliberate result of Step 3, not an error.
